# OKRs & KPIs — Reusable Template

> **How to use this template**
> This document holds two different things — keep them distinct:
> - **OKRs** (Objectives & Key Results): a few ambitious goals that drive *change* this period. Time-boxed (usually a quarter). Aspirational.
> - **KPIs**: always-on metrics that track the *steady-state health* of the product/business. Not time-boxed.
>
> **They are complementary, not interchangeable.** A KPI dropping might *inspire* an OKR to fix it. An OKR is the change; the KPI is the dial you watch.
>
> | | OKRs | KPIs |
> |---|---|---|
> | Purpose | Drive change toward big goals | Monitor ongoing performance |
> | Timeframe | A cycle (quarter) | Continuous |
> | Ambition | Stretch ("a little scary") | Realistic targets / thresholds |
> | Count | Few (focus) | As many as needed to stay healthy |
>
> **Discipline rules (from Doerr & Wodtke's *Radical Focus*)**
> - **Few OKRs.** Max 3–5 objectives, each with 2–4 key results. When in doubt, fewer. Ideally one objective is *the* focus.
> - **Doerr's formula:** "We will **[Objective]** as measured by **[Key Results]**."
> - **Objectives** are qualitative, inspiring, memorable. **Key Results** are measurable *outcomes*, not tasks/outputs (not "ship X" — rather "X causes Y").
> - **KRs should be a little scary** — if you're 100% sure you'll hit them, aim higher.
> - **OKRs are a learning & alignment tool — NOT a performance-review weapon.** Misused, they do more harm than good.
> - **Living document.** Check in weekly, score confidence monthly, grade at cycle end.
> - Delete this block and italic *prompts* once written.

## B4. Anti-Patterns Checklist (review before committing)
- [ ] Are any KRs actually tasks/outputs ("launch X") instead of outcomes? Fix them.
- [ ] Too many objectives? Cut to the vital few.
- [ ] Are KRs measurable with a clear baseline & target?
- [ ] Are any OKRs really just business-as-usual KPIs in disguise? Move them to §4.
- [ ] Are these being used to evaluate individuals? Stop — that breaks the system.
- [ ] Are they all sandbagged (sure to hit)? Add stretch.
