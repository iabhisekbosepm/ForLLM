# Persona Profiles — Reusable Template

> **How to use this template**
> A persona is a **research document representing a real segment of your users or buyers** — not a poster on a wall. It exists to help the team make decisions by understanding who they're building for and what those people are trying to achieve.
> - **PART A — Core**: the persona library overview + a profile block you copy per persona.
> - **PART B — Extended**: deeper research, journey, and segmentation for high-stakes decisions.
>
> **Principles (Cooper's goal-directed design + JTBD + modern practice)**
> - **Goals first.** Organize each persona around what they're trying to accomplish (their Jobs-to-be-Done). Everything else explains that goal and what blocks it.
> - **Ground it in research.** Build from interviews, analytics, surveys, support data — and log your sources. Modern personas favor **role, JTBD, behaviors, and decision criteria** over demographics.
> - **Label the confidence:** **Proto-persona** (team assumptions — fine early, but validate later) vs. **Research-based** (validated with real users — the default for costly decisions).
> - **Separate buyer from user** where they differ (critical in B2B).
> - **Keep it to 3–5 personas** covering your highest-value segments. Start with 2–3; expand deliberately.
> - **Living document.** Revisit as you learn. Delete this block and italic *prompts* once written.
