# Competitive Analysis — Reusable Template

> **How to use this template**
> A competitive analysis exists to drive a decision, not to collect facts. Every section should ladder up to **"so what should *we* do?"**
> - **PART A — Core**: fill in for every analysis. Keep it focused.
> - **PART B — Extended**: deeper tools (Five Forces, win/loss, battlecards) for strategic planning, fundraising, or sales enablement.
>
> **Discipline rules (from best practice)**
> - **Cap depth at ~6 competitors.** Focus beats breadth. List the rest, analyze the few that matter.
> - **Classify every competitor:** Direct (same product, same audience) / Indirect (same problem, different solution) / Potential (could enter).
> - **Living document.** Light monitoring (launches, pricing) continuously; deep refresh quarterly or after a major competitor move (funding, launch, acquisition).
> - **Cite sources** for every claim — competitive intel goes stale and gets repeated as fact. Mark guesses as `[ASSUMPTION]`.
> - If a section doesn't apply, write "N/A — because…". Delete this block and italic *prompts* once written.
