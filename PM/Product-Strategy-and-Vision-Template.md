# Product Strategy & Vision — Reusable Template

> **How to use this template**
> This document captures *why your product exists* (Vision) and *how you'll win* (Strategy). They are different things — keep them distinct.
> - **PART A — Core**: the sections you fill in for every product. Keep it lean and focused.
> - **PART B — Extended**: deeper sections for fundraising, annual planning, or large/complex products.
>
> **The hierarchy this doc lives in** *(top = stable & long-term, bottom = changes often)*:
> `Mission → Vision (2–5 yrs) → Strategy (how) → Roadmap (6–12 mo) → Backlog`
> This template covers **Mission → Vision → Strategy**. The roadmap and backlog live elsewhere.
>
> **Rules of thumb**
> - **Vision is the destination; strategy is the route.** Vision changes rarely; strategy adapts as you learn.
> - **Focus is the whole point of strategy** — what you say *no* to matters as much as what you pursue.
> - Ground the vision in real customer needs and market reality, not just ambition.
> - This is a *living document*. Revisit strategy quarterly, vision yearly.
> - If a section doesn't apply, write "N/A — because…". Delete this block and the italic *prompts* once written.
